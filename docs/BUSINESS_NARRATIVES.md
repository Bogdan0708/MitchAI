## PRIORITIZATION RATIONALE

### Why This Order:

1. **Days 1-30 (Validation):** Nothing else matters if operators won't pay. Prove commercial viability before any feature expansion.

2. **Days 31-60 (Iteration):** Early adopters surface critical friction. Fix those before scaling acquisition spend.
