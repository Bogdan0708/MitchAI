# Mitch Hospitality Platform
## Sales Presentation Guide

*For presenting to restaurant owners, managers, and hospitality business decision-makers.*

---

## Opening (2 minutes)

### The Hook
> "What if your restaurant could serve customers 24/7, respond to every review within minutes, and free up your staff to focus on what matters most — creating exceptional dining experiences?"

### The Problem
Every restaurant faces the same challenges:
- Staff stretched thin answering phones and messages
- Reviews going unanswered (hurting your reputation)
- Missed reservations and orders outside business hours
- Time wasted on repetitive admin tasks
- Rising costs with shrinking margins

### The Solution
**Mitch** is an AI-powered platform built specifically for restaurants. It handles customer communication, manages reviews, and automates daily operations — so you can focus on food and hospitality.

## Handling Objections

### "We already use [X] for reservations/orders"
> "Mitch integrates with the tools you already use. It's not about replacing what works — it's about adding intelligence on top."

### "AI won't understand our business"
> "You train the AI with your specific menu, policies, and FAQs. It learns your voice and your values. Plus, anything complex gets escalated to your team."

### "What about data privacy?"
> "Your data stays yours. We're fully GDPR compliant, and we never share customer information with third parties."

### "Is it complicated to set up?"
> "Most restaurants are up and running within a day. We handle the technical setup, and our team is here to help every step of the way."

### "What if customers want to talk to a real person?"
> "The AI knows when to step back. Any request for a human, or any complex issue, is immediately flagged and handed to your staff."
