## ⚠️ KEY CONSIDERATIONS FOR GRILL CONCEPT

1. **Extraction is CRITICAL**
   - Charcoal/grill produces heavy smoke
   - Most dark kitchens have electric extraction but may not handle charcoal
   - Ask specifically about charcoal/open flame permissions

2. **Planning Permission**
   - Camden is strict (full Sui Generis consent required)
   - Westminster/Tower Hamlets faster for registration

3. **Not Kosher/Not Halal**
   - Some shared kitchens have religious dietary restrictions
   - Verify there are no cross-contamination rules that would prevent pork/mixed meat

4. **Delivery Platforms**
   - Already have Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat accounts from Commercial Street
   - Can reactivate with new kitchen address
